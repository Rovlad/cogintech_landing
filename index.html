We already have an IDMS. Does this still help?+

Yes. The audit reads from your existing system. It examines whether sampled values can be traced to source documents and where engineering effort goes. Your IDMS stays the system of record; nothing is installed or written back.

Can our data stay within our company?+

Yes. Work can run in a tenancy you control using a redacted export, or on site by separate agreement. The data route and NDA are agreed before the audit begins. Exported copies are deleted and access revoked at close unless you request otherwise.

What if we are not ready for AI?+

That is a useful finding. You receive the time-value maps, data readiness findings, and evidence of what needs repair first. If nothing justifies automation, the recommendation is to stop—with the reasoning documented.

What does the fixed fee cover?+

Three workflows mapped, one examined in depth, over ten working days: a report, read-out, and working files. The fee is quoted once the workflows and scope are agreed, with 50% at kick-off and 50% at read-out. Additional investigation, data remediation, and implementation are separate, optional engagements.

Why a fixed price?+

Because the scope is fixed: three workflows, one traced to source, ten days. A defined scope and fixed deliverables let us quote the full engagement upfront. No day rates, no software to install.

How is this different from a conventional AI assessment?+

It combines operator-side integrity assurance experience with hands-on AI application. We assess engineering workflows, source-data reliability, and the controls required for safe deployment—not just automation opportunities.
