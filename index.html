Signs this audit is for you

AI can give integrity engineers their time back. The challenge is knowing where it pays off.

Not every workflow is ready for AI, and not every automation creates measurable value. These are the situations where it is worth finding out — before you invest.

  • Engineers handle data more than they assess integrity

    Searching for reports, re-keying values into the IDMS, checking the same information twice.

  • IDMS values cannot be traced to source documents

    Thickness, material and design data scattered across systems and hard to trace back.

  • RBA / RBI cycles take months

    Most of the effort goes into assembling and checking information, not assessing risk.

  • The anomaly backlog grows despite real effort

    Nobody can say how many hours it would take to clear.

  • Manual data handling erodes margins

    Operators pay for hours spent searching and checking; service providers lose profitability on fixed-fee delivery.

  • Management wants an AI plan nobody can quantify

    Where to apply AI first, how much effort it could save, and what data or assurance gaps stand in the way.

Questions we are usually asked

Before you book the call

We already have an IDMS and a data-quality programme.

Good — the audit reads from it. Conformance checks tell you a value is in the right format; they do not tell you whether the document behind it agrees. That second question is the one an AI tool will trip on.

Our data cannot leave the company.

It does not have to. We work in a tenancy you control on a redacted export, or on site by separate agreement.

We are not ready for AI yet.

That is a finding, not a reason to wait. The audit tells you how far off you are in hours and records, what to repair first and what it is worth.

What if you find nothing worth automating?

Then you get the time-value map, the trust register and a written "stop" with the evidence.

Why a fixed price?

Because the scope is fixed: three workflows, one traced to source, ten days. A defined scope and fixed deliverables let us quote the full engagement upfront. No day rates, no software to install.

How is this different from a conventional process-improvement or AI assessment?

It combines operator-side integrity assurance experience with hands-on AI application. We assess engineering workflows, source-data reliability and the controls required for safe deployment — not just automation opportunities.
